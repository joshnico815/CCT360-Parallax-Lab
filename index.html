2005

From Japan to North American

UNIQLO enters the U.S. market, bringing its first store to New Jersey. This expansion was an early experiment to test the brand's impact on a different audience.

2006

New York City

Uniqlo opens its SoHo flagship in New York City. The store introduced American consumers to its fast-high fashion approach to the industry and consistently promoted the brand through New York advertising.

2011

The Fifth Avenue Global Flagship

Uniqlo opens its huge global flagship on Fifth Avenue in New York, highlighting the brand's success in the U.S. market. This established UNIQLO's position as an emerging powerhouse in the fashion industry in one of the world's most visible fashion markets.

2016

Canada

Uniqlo finally buries its seeds in Canada, opening its first Canadian store at Toronto Eaton Centre and its second store at Yorkdale Shopping Centre. Canada plays a crucial role for the success of UNIQLO's expansion in North American.

Late 10s-Early 20s

Enhance Brand Awareness

Rather than attempting to find more success elsewhere, UNIQLO focuses on building and strengthening its brand awareness in North America by pushes its LifeWear identity, emphasizing products from its newest innovations; HEATTECH, AIRism, fleece, and everyday basics.

2023-2024

American Success

As growth in North America accelerates, UNIQLO opens its first store in Texas, where five Houston and Dallas-area stores opened in fall 2024. For Uniqlo's 2024 fiscal year earning in North American, the brand's revenue reached ¥217.7 billion, up 32.8%, while operating profit rose 65.1%.

2025

Sales, Sales, Sales, & More Sales

Uniqlo's expansion becomes much more aggressive. From its viral design to innovative products, Uniqlo's North American revenue increases another 24.5% to ¥271.1 billion, with business profit rising 35.1% to ¥44.2 billion. Fast Retailing says successful new stores are contributing to increasing its brand awareness and online sales.

2026

Fashion Powerhouse

As of 2026, UNIQLO has 86 U.S. stores and 38 Canadian stores, amounting to 124 stores in North America. Its current goal now is to establish major flagship stores in regions with growing customers and then surround them with additional locations.
